Fit to Content and Text Fit

Translated text is rarely the length of the text it replaces. German labels run about a third longer than English ones, and a button sized for "Options" cuts off "Einstellungen". Game UI handles this in two ways. Fit to content lets a button, a panel or a text take the size its content needs, between a minimum and a maximum. Text fit finds every text that still does not fit, in every language, while you edit and when the asset is transformed.

Size modes

Every element has a SizeMode. Fixed, the default, keeps the authored size from the rect transform. Fit content measures the element's content and uses that size instead, clamped to MinSize and MaxSize.

  • A text measures its lines in its font, at its font size, with its letter spacing and line height.
  • A stack panel measures its children along its direction, with spacing, margins and padding.
  • Any other element measures its children, with their offsets.
  • Images, empty panels, grids and sub-documents keep their authored size.

Fitting applies only on axes where the element is not stretched. An element anchored at a point (both anchors equal on that axis) grows around its pivot: a button anchored at its centre grows to both sides, one anchored at its left edge grows to the right. A stretched axis always follows its parent.

Inside a stack panel the child's size along the stack comes from the same measurement, so a vertical menu of Fit content buttons lays itself out as the labels change. The measured text is the text the element shows: its binding, its translation in the previewed language, or the pseudo-localised text.

Note
A MaxSize of 0 on an axis means no limit on that axis. A text that wraps needs a MaxSize width: it wraps at that width and grows in height.

Making a button fit its label

  1. Select the button, or the text inside it, on the canvas or in the hierarchy.
  2. In the Size panel, set Size to Fit content.
  3. Drag Min width and Max width to the limits the design allows. When the element has no limits yet, switching to Fit content sets a minimum width of the current width (at most 120 px), a minimum height of the current height, and a maximum width of 2.5 times the current width (at least 420 px).
  4. Pick an Overflow mode for the label, for the case where the text is longer than the maximum.

The Needs row shows the width the label needs against the width it has, red when it does not fit. A drag on a slider is one undo step.

Fit all buttons

Fit all buttons in the canvas toolbar switches every button and toggle of the document to Fit content with the default limits above. It is one undo step. Limits a button already has are kept.

Overflow modes

ModeWhat a text that is too long does
OverflowDraws past its rect.
ClipIs cut at its rect.
EllipsisThe last line that fits ends in "...".
Shrink to FitSteps its font size down, to no less than MinFontSize, until it fits. A text that does not fit even at the minimum is truncated with "...".

The Text Fit panel

The Text Fit panel lists every text of the document that does not fit in the language the canvas previews. Its header reads, for example, TEXT FIT · 3 in Deutsch, or · all fit. Each row names the control and the problem:

RowMeaning
draws past its rectOverflow mode, and the text is longer than its rect.
is clippedClip mode, and part of the text is cut off.
is ellipsizedEllipsis mode, or Shrink to Fit even at the minimum font size.
shrinks to N pxShrink to Fit found a size that fits. Informational.
N characters missing from FontThe texts use characters the font has no glyph for. They draw as boxes. One row per font.

Click a row to select its control. A red dot marks each text that does not fit at the top right of its rect on the canvas. The canvas status row sums it up: "3 overflowing · 12 fit to content".

Checking a language

  1. Choose the language in the Language strip of the canvas toolbar. Languages are the folders of <project>/Localization (and Editor/Localization). Their names show in their own language when the editor font can draw them.
  2. Read the Text Fit panel. Fix each row: Fit content, a wider rect, Shrink to Fit, or a shorter translation.
  3. Choose Pseudo +40% to test texts that have no translation yet. Every text shows lengthened by 40% with accented letters ("[Öþţîöñš ~~~]"), which shows both truncation and missing glyphs before any translation exists.

Missing characters

A missing-characters row has an Add characters button. It adds the missing characters, and every character the previewed language's string tables use, to the font's CustomCharacters, then saves and transforms the font. The change is one undo step in the font asset.

Transform warnings

Transforming a UI Document checks its texts at 1920 × 1080, once with the authored texts and once per language of the project. Each text that does not fit is a warning in the log, such as Text fit (de): Btn_Options: ellipsized, needs 212 px, has 170 px, at most 20 per language. A shrunk text that fits is not a warning. Editing a string table re-transforms the documents.

The asset browser shows assets whose last transform had warnings with the warning badge. The Asset Curator panel lists them under Warnings and shows the warnings in the asset's details.
